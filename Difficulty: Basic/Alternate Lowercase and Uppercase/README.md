<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/crazy-string1157/1">Alternate Lowercase and Uppercase</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Basic</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);" bis_skin_checked="1"><p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given a string <strong>s</strong>, convert it into an alternating sequence of <strong>lowercase</strong> and <strong>uppercase</strong> characters while keeping the character at index <strong>0</strong> unchanged. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Return the modified string.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>s = "geeksforgeeks"<strong>
Output: </strong>gEeKsFoRgEeKs<strong>
Explanation: </strong>The first character is lowercase, so characters at even indices remain lowercase and characters at odd indices are converted to uppercase.</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>s = "Geeksforgeeks"<strong>
Output: </strong>GeEkSfOrGeEkS<strong>
Explanation: </strong>The first character is uppercase, so characters at even indices remain uppercase and characters at odd indices are converted to lowercase.</span></span></pre>
