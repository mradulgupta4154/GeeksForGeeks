<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/count-the-characters-in-each-word-in-a-given-sentence3451/1">Word Lengths</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Basic</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);" bis_skin_checked="1"><p><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Given a sentence<strong> s</strong> consisting of words formed using English letters and separated by a single space, return an array containing the length of each word in the order they appear in the sentence.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input: </strong>s = "the quick brown fox"
<strong>Output:</strong> 3 5 5 3
<strong>Explanation</strong>: The words are "the", "quick", "brown", and "fox", whose lengths are 3, 5, 5, and 3 respectively.
</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input</strong>: s = "geeks for geeks"
<strong>Output:</strong> 5 3 5
<strong>Explanation</strong>: The words are "geeks", "for", and "geeks", whose lengths are 5, 3, and 5 respectively.
</span></pre>
